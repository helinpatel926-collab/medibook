Questions

Can I book for my child?

Yes. Choose Pediatrics and enter your child's name and date of birth. Pediatrics sees patients under 18.

How do I cancel or change my appointment?

Call the front desk with your booking reference. We ask for at least 24 hours' notice so someone else can use the time.

Do I need a referral?

Not for Family Medicine, Pediatrics or Dermatology. Cardiology and Orthopedics usually need a referral from your family doctor; bring it to your visit.

What if I'm running late?

Call us. If you're more than 15 minutes late, we may need to move you to the next open time.
